Indigenous Aerospace Technologies Built in India

At SPACECORPZ AEROSPACE PRIVATE LIMITED, we’re working with one goal in mind to build our own Indian-made aerospace systems that truly serve people & industries. Right now, we’re developing our multipurpose unmanned & manned aerial vehicle system that’s fully designed & built in India. It’s made to work in real Indian conditions & to support areas like farming, logistics, infrastructure, public safety & defense.

We’re building multipurpose unmanned and manned aerial vehicle systems engineered entirely in India, capable of performing in real Indian conditions supporting agriculture, logistics, infrastructure, public safety, and defense.

Collaboration with local manufacturers, engineers, and research institutions defines our process. We believe innovation grows when knowledge stays within the nation, empowering every Indian hand that builds it.
