About Us

Building India's aerospace future designed, developed, and made in India.

Our Story

We started SPACECORPZ AEROSPACE PRIVATE LIMITED with one clear dream to make India self-reliant in aerospace technology. From the very beginning, we’ve believed our country has the talent and courage to design & build its own advanced systems.

Our purpose is to create Indian-made aerospace products that can serve real needs in agriculture, logistics, infrastructure, disaster response, and national safety.

We’re a small team with big determination. We’ve spent years learning, designing, and testing ideas that can truly help people & industries. Every part of our work is developed in India with our own effort and strong collaboration with Indian manufacturers, local suppliers, and technical experts.

Our first project is an intelligent unmanned aerial system being built completely in India. It’s designed to work in real Indian conditions and to deliver reliable performance for both public & commercial uses.

SPACECORPZ AEROSPACE PRIVATE LIMITED was founded in Kochi, Kerala with a strong goal of promoting Indigenous Aerospace Manufacturing & Exporting. We’re deeply committed to working hand in hand with Indian & local manufacturers to build a trusted supply chain that supports our nation’s growth in aerospace.

We believe technology should grow from our soil and fly under our own flag. With every step forward, we carry the same spirit of Indian innovation, Indian capability, and Indian pride.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To design and manufacture advanced, indigenous aerospace systems that empower India’s technological independence and serve critical needs across diverse sectors.

🌍

Our Vision
