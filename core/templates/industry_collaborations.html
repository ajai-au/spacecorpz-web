INDUSTRY & RESEARCH COLLABORATIONS


At SPACECORPZ AEROSPACE PRIVATE LIMITED, we believe India’s real strength is in its people, knowledge & teamwork. We’re building our company on collaboration, not competition. Our aim is to bring together Indian manufacturers, suppliers, designers, skilled engineers & research groups who share the same dream of developing our own aerospace technologies.

We’re looking to collaborate with industries & research organisations that can support the growth of Indigenous Aerospace Manufacturing & Exporting. We welcome partnerships with component makers, structure manufacturers, system developers, & technology solution providers from across India.

We’re also open to working with government agencies, research centres & universities to share knowledge & explore new ideas in aerospace development. Our team truly values collaboration with institutions like the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), Defence Research & Development Organisation (DRDO), National Aerospace Laboratories (NAL), and other national research bodies that represent India’s scientific excellence.

We invite scientists, engineers, innovators & students who believe in Indian innovation to join us in creating a stronger aerospace ecosystem. Together, we can build the future of Indian aerospace — made in India, designed in India & built for the world.
