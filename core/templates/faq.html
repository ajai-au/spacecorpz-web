Do I need a license to operate an SPACECORPZ drone?

+

Licensing requirements vary by country and use case:
• USA: Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate for commercial use
• EU: EU Drone Competency Certificate required
• Recreational use: Basic registration may be required
• We provide guidance on local regulations and compliance requirements
• Our training programs include regulatory education

What are the flight restrictions I should know about?

+

Common flight restrictions include:
• No-fly zones around airports and military bases
• Maximum altitude limits (typically 400ft/120m)
• Visual line of sight requirements
• Privacy considerations over private property
• Weather and visibility limitations
• Our mobile app includes real-time airspace information and restrictions
